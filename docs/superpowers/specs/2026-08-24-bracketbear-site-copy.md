## /ABOUT — the standards page

- **Title:** What we hold ourselves to.
- **Subtitle:** Three commitments, and the work that stands behind them.

### Why this exists

> Bracket Bear exists because the platform layer is where experiential work
> quietly goes wrong. Not the idea and not the design — the wiring underneath. A
> CMS nobody wants to open. An integration written for one install and never
> again. A content change that takes four people and a site visit.
>
> Every hour spent there is an hour not spent on what the client actually hired
> you for. We rebuild that layer so it stops taking the hours.
>
> Rooted in Pittsburgh. Based in Portland.

### Three commitments

**C / 01 — Community**

> **Lifting up the people coming after us.**
>
> Somebody made room for us when we needed it. We make room for who's next —
> apprentices, junior engineers, people learning the platform layer for the
> first time. That's how we got here, and it's how the work keeps getting
> better.

**C / 02 — Collaboration**

> **Co-owned, including the messy parts.**
>
> We share credit and we share the messy parts. We stay close enough to the work
> that it ends up co-owned, because it is.

**C / 03 — Continuous improvement**

> **Question our own work first.**
>
> We're harder on our own systems than anyone else is. The platform that runs
> Bracket Bear gets the same scrutiny we'd put on a client's: the invoices, the
> repo, the docs, the way we run a meeting. The day that stops, the work stops
> getting better.

> C/02 was: _"we don't show up like a vendor running a checklist."_ That is the
> banned "look at us NOT doing X" frame. Restated declaratively.
>
> The third C is **Continuous improvement**, not Consistency. Live `/about` says
> Consistency — "we sweat the details… every time" — a virtue too small to flex
> on, which `BRAND.md` bans by name.
