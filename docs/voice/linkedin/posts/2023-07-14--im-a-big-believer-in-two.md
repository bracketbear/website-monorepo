I'm a big believer in two things when it comes to urban infrastructure:

1. We need to keep rent affordable and available
2. We should encourage and incentivize walking, biking, and using public transportation as much as possible while also discouraging car use as much as possible

I bring that up because I've heard a lot about how hybrid and remote work has killed the demand for office space. I wonder if there would be benefit to converting some of that office space into liveable space? This is genuine wonder, because I personally feel that adding more liveable space to business districts would encourage lower rent, safer streets due to folks being there all the time, and new business popping up to support the families that live there.
