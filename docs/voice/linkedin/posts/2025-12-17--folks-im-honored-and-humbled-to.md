Folks, I'm honored and humbled to share with you all that last week I was presented with a Community Award from Fireside. 🥹

In February, I saw on Instagram that Bryant Place would be giving a presentation at a creative technology meetup, and my interest was piqued. A creative tech meetup in town? Awesome - sign me up!

So I show up, watch the incredible presentations, and get to chatting with Terence, the organizer of the event. I’m immediately hooked. The vision was clear: build and highlight the creative technology community in Portland. As someone who had been working in the field for five years, I felt like this was something I could contribute to by leveraging my professional connections and helping grow our group’s resources.

To top it all off, Bryant’s presentation was about how the CMS software I build adds value to immersive experiences. I was so ready to Kool-Aid Man myself through the wall and build the best damn experiential CMS I possibly could.

The very next month, I was laid off from that job...

Next Fireside in April, I show up, unemployed and lost. Frankly, I didn't feel like I had anything to contribute anymore.

However, being at Fireside really kept my soul warm. While I was there, I saw folks from all sorts of creative backgrounds coming together to learn and build - but more importantly, I saw people supporting one another. The community was there to act as an emotional safety net for folks who needed it.

So I’m telling you all this to say: this award means a hell of a lot more to me than I can begin to express. It represents a year that started about as rough as it could and is ending with uncertainty, but also with copious amounts of hope.

## I'm not crying, you're crying.

While I have your attention, Fireside will be looking for a few things next year as we continue to grow as a community:

1. We're looking for presenters!
   If you’ve got wisdom to share, we’d love to hear it. We’re especially excited to have PDX-based presenters speak at our in-person Fireside meetups, but we’ll also be starting virtual meetups so anyone can be part of the group. If you want to chat about creative ideas, technology, AI, business - or even how to run a group like ours - let’s freaking talk and get you in. Plus, it’s a great way to put yourself out there and add presenting to your resume.

2. We're looking for corporate sponsors!
   Sponsorships start at $50/month and really help us make these events happen. We’ll work with you and your organization to make sure you’re getting the most bang for your buck. Don't let Bracket Bear beat y'all to the punch here.

3. We're looking for other venues to host the event at.
   Ideally, we’re looking for a place with a big ol’ LED wall (potentially called a “Big Wall?”) that we can rip some presentations and TouchDesigner projects on. Maybe somewhere around NW 16th & Johnson? 🤷🧐
