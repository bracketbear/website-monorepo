YES! This video really speaks to me.

It’s easy for developers to feel like they’re not being productive if they aren’t actively writing code, but it’s important to recognize when you’re not in the zone and to embrace it.

Grab a coffee, go for a walk, or reinforce a skill that you know needs work. Whatever you do, don’t force productive work if it’s just not time for it.

Find inspiration and the focused energy will follow.
