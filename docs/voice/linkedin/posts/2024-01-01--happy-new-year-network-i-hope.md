Happy New Year, network! I hope that you're already crushing 2024. 💪

2023 was a freaking wild year for me. In a year of mass layoffs and job uncertainty, I chose to voluntarily leave my job so I could try to find what makes me happy at work and in life. Here are three HUGE lessons I learned last year:

💰 Money doesn't matter as much to me as it used to. Okay, I know that sounds grossly altruistic but here me out. I had always felt like I'd been behind where I "should be" as far as my salary goes, and this year was when I realized that, hey, it's not all about the Benjamins. Love what you do. Speaking of which...

🎨 What I'm working on matters to me. I need to be inspired by the products I build and support. Working in experiential technology was a blessing and I didn't realize how much I loved it until I left it to work in e-commerce. And about work...

🫣 Freelancing might not be for me. There, I said it. I'm so pumped that some people are able to do it successfully, but gosh darn is it hard, time consuming, and often thankless. Plus, I just don't want to make a website for your uncle's business.

In 2014, I was managing the front-of-house of a microbrewery in rural Pennsylvania trying to figure out where to go from there. Tomorrow I start a new job at a company that I've wanted to work at for years. What a life.

See you folks in 2024! 🎉
