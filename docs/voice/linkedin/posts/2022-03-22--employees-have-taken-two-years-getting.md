Employees have taken two years getting used to this new normal.

We have built offices.

We have established routines.

Preparing for work, commuting, and having butts-in-seats just doesn’t seem worth it for a lot of people.

We should be focusing on policies that encourage employees doing their best work, not to justify overhead.
