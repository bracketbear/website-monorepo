If you haven’t seen experiential software come to life on production hardware for the first time, you haven’t lived.

No, seriously. There’s no time to wait.

Go quit your job now. Take out student loans. Graduate with honors from NYU. Break into the industry. Work with your new teammates deep in the intersection of design and technology, building bespoke software that pushes the limits of how man and machine interact.

Then, when you’re on site and surrounded by your peers and clients, waiting with baited breath as you toil and configure for hours, know that it’ll all be worth it if you can just get everything to work in-sync.

Wait — was that what I think it was?

Did everything finally start working like it always has on your local machine?

That beating in your chest isn’t stress or excitement. No, it’s so much more than that.

Congratulations. This is what you’ve been working towards for years.

Now you’re truly alive.
