Last night I went to my first creative technology meetup, Fireside, where I got to see Bryant Place, a creative technologist and former teammate of mine at Downstream, give a great presentation on content management systems (CMS) and how they can be used to enhance your generative vision.

I couldn’t have asked for a better topic for my first meetup and it got me freaking STOKED. Here’s why:

I’ve been fortunate enough to work on multiple experiential CMSs over the past five years. Empowering creatives to unlock their vision is precisely what drives me to help build the Gumband platform. To hear an established creative’s perspective on the value of content management was wildly motivating—it was so hard to not start yelling and fist-pumping while Bryant described the true power of the tools we build and use.

If you haven’t been to Fireside yet, I’d highly recommend it. The group meets every two months at Goodies Snack Shop in Portland and is looking to grow. Next meetup they’ll be talking about Comfy UI and other AI-related topics—I won’t be missing it. Ping me or comment below if you have any questions. Their Instagram page is here:
