“If you’re not first, you’re last.” - Ricky Bobby

I think it’s hard for non-developers to wrap their head around the importance of encouraging learning during work because most professions don’t require you to continuously learn.

Want to know what it’s like to be a developer?

Read blogs, news articles, tutorials, documentation, create projects, make a portfolio, go to a conference, learn a new piece of technology…

Okay, but for how long?

Until you’re not a developer.

One reason why developers are valuable is because we also have to be perpetually learning in order to succeed in our jobs.

Many careers don’t have to do that.

Encourage learning at work! 🧐
