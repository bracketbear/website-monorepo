“Quality means rethinking, reworking, and polishing. [Students] need to feel they will be celebrated, not ridiculed, for going back to the drawing board.”

- Ron Berger, teacher and author

This was a thought-provoking quote that I just read in Adam Grant’s book “Think Again.” This concept can totally be applied to work teams of all types.
