Another reason why I dig Deeplocal: the branding, a.k.a., the 'tude.

While branding trends have become stale, flat, and, well, rather corporate over the years, Deeplocal's new brand is this expressive skull dude that I can't get enough of. Sure, this could have been a lowercase "dl" in Montserrat font with a slight blue gradient applied to it (not saying I've ever branded a company that way... but if I did, you might be able to see it in my work history 🙈), but instead they chose to make their brand a character WITH character. This immediately represents what I feel when I think of them: creative, polished, and slightly rebellious. 🤘

Can't wait for people to creep on my LinkedIn profile and see this little buddy staring back at them. The world needs more of this.
