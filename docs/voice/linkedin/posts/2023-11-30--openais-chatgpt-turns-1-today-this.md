OpenAI's ChatGPT turns 1 today! 🎉 This got me thinking about how much it has impacted my life so far:

✅ It has made me a much better developer. Being able to pair program with an LLM that has extensive knowledge of best practices and has a solid understanding of programming is immensely valuable.

✅ It has made me a significantly more marketable job candidate. I was able to produce the best resume I've ever had by bouncing ideas off of it and comparing my skills with what jobs are looking for.

✅ Finally, it has given me a new creative outlet. I love using it to generate images with DALL-E 3, write stories about ridiculous nonsense, and anything else I can think of to keep me occupied.

If there's one thing that I've learned, it's that it's ultimately still a tool and NOT a replacement. In other words, I don't assume that the code it writes is great, nor do I just take the resume advice that it gives me as gospel. In fact, a lot of times it's kinda crappy. However, I've learned how to use it as an intelligent, supremely well educated, and non-judgemental partner in a lot of the tasks that I do every single day.

Here's to the future. 🥂
