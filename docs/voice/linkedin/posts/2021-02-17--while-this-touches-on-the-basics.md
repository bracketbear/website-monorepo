While this touches on the basics (plan your day the night before, don’t reach for your cellphone first thing, etc.), I think that the third point is one that I use all the time:

Different problems require different routines.

Personally, my routine for a software development day tends to be more of a traditional workday. However, if I’m trying to be creative, my desk is the last place you’ll find me. In fact, I’ll probably do a lot of walks around the neighborhood and then spend my day thinking at a coffee shop (oh, I miss coffee shops).

What are your routines like and do they change based on your goals for the day?
