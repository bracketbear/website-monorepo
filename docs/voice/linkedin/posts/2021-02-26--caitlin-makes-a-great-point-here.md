Caitlin makes a great point here.

Asking someone, “Where do you see yourself in five years?” is a tricky and, in my opinion, a loaded question with no good answer 99% of the time. The retort might be that they see themselves in the hiring manager’s position and, if that offends you as the hiring manager, then you shouldn’t have asked.

While I think it is a somewhat cliche response, this response also tells me that this person has goals that extend beyond the position that they’re interviewing for and they shouldn’t be punished for it.

Does anyone have a good answer for this that they’ve used or heard? I’d love to hear them.
