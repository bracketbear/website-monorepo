I don't normally geek out about tooling, but Vite is game changing.

Javascript used to be a simple scripting language, but modern development workflows require complex tooling.

Complex tooling requires elaborate setup steps.

Elaborate setup steps require immense amounts of time... plus, people judge you for not knowing how to set up Webpack from memory. (SMH...)

You see where I'm going with this.

Vite just helped me spin up a new app with Typescript, a dev server, and a bundler... and it only took me 30 seconds!

Oh, and the bundler is FAST A.F.!

Bye-bye, Webpack. I'd say that I'll miss you but I never liked you in the first place.
