When I had employees, I always made sure to truly support their future endeavors, whether it would be a with us or with someone else. I didn’t care what or where it was.

Why?

I always want people to succeed, and it’s foolish to think that your employees will be able to fulfill all of their goals at your company.

As an employer, I believe that it’s your job to help your employees find their wings.

Don’t be hurt when it’s time for them to fly.
