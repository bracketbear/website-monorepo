I’ve always found our society’s fear of talking about pay… bizarre.

If my parents would have talked to me about it, I would have had a better understanding of what a “good salary” is.

If colleges talked about what you can realistically expect to earn with one of their degrees, students would be able to make better career decisions (or just not go to college in many cases… 🤷🏻‍♂️).

If companies are open about pay, they can make an impact on truly making pay equal for women, non-white folks, and LBGTQ+ people.

How can people make informed decisions if they aren’t informed?

Let’s start being more open about pay, folks. 👏
