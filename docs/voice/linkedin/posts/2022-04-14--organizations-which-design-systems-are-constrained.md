"Organizations which design systems are constrained to produce designs which are copies of the communication structures of these organizations."

- Melvin Conway, "How do Committees Invent?"

I read this in The Pragmatic Programmer the other day and I thought that it was worth sharing.

The gist of the quote is that the quality of the application that you're developing is a direct reflection of the quality of the communication in your organization. Good communication makes good products and bad communication leads to bad ones. It's that simple.

That was also from 1967… 🤯
