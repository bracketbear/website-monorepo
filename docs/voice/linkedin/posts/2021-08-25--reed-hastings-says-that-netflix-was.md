Reed Hastings says that Netflix was built on a “culture of candor.”

Being open and honest is the only way to truly identify what’s broken and to grow stronger, whether that’s identifying a personal weakness or workplace pain points.

How else do we grow if we aren’t being truthful with ourselves?
