Designer friends:

Serif, the folks behind Affinity Designer, Photo, and Publisher, are offering their software at $24.99 per license due to the ongoing pandemic. I’ve used Designer and Publisher as alternatives to their Adobe CC peers and I think they’re well worth the look.

This is a great example of corporate leadership during hard times.
