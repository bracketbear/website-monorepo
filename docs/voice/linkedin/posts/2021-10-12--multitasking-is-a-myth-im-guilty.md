Multitasking is a myth!

(I’m guilty of it myself, too. 😔)

It’s important to remember that your brain needs time to focus on one thing in order to really give it your full attention.

According to this article, your brain needs anywhere from 5-25 minutes to focus back to the task at hand after being distracted.

HOLY COW!

What can we do to try to mitigate this?

I’ve been trying to turn off Teams when I need to get work done (I know there’s a “do not disturb” mode, but I still check it every five minutes…).

I also try to keep my phone ten feet away from me to keep the distractions at bay.
