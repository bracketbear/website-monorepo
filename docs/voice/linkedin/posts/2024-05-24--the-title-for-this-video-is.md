The title for this video is pretty cringeworthy, but I think that the overall message is stellar: Make sure that you're always striving to be as hospitable as you possibly can. Like, to a ridiculous extreme.

The argument here is that if two different services are equivalent in almost every way, the differentiator might be how personalized of an experience the consumer is offered. You can't just give just good service - remember that the idea is to go beyond expectation. Leave your consumer with an experience they'll never forget.

Any service that's provided can benefit. Restaurants are the example that Will Guidara uses to lay the foundation for the discussion since it's probably what most of us think of when we think of hospitality, but this mentality could easily be applied to how you manage your people, how you interact with your coworkers, how you interact with folks online... you catch my drift.

This video is well worth the five minutes it takes to watch.
