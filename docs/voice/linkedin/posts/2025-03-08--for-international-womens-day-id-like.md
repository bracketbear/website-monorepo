For International Women's Day, I'd like to highlight one of the most impactful humans to ever have touched my career: Tessa Ribitsch.

A little over five years ago, Tessa gave me a huge opportunity doing contract work at Downstream in a position I'm sure more qualified people also applied for. I don't know why she took a chance on me, but her generosity opened my eyes to an industry that I didn't know existed and that I would end up falling in love with.

Six months later, as the pandemic was in full-swing and I was sitting at home on my couch, she gave me another opportunity—this time an offer to be a full-time creative coder, all while knowing I didn't know a lick of C++ but also aware that I'd bust my tush to learn it. Her belief in my potential, not my portfolio, put me in a position where I was able to learn a lower level programming language, which eventually shed the imposter syndrome that I had been carrying around with me for years.

It's impossible to look at my work history and not see the direct impact Tessa has made on my career and my life. Without her, I wouldn't have ever worked at Deeplocal. Heck, I wouldn't have even known that this industry existed. And to be perfectly frank, I don't think I'd even be programming still. There's a good chance I'd be tomato farming if I wasn't working around and supporting creatives making cool shit.

Tessa, you're a rockstar, a ninja, a [insert dorky LinkedIn title here]... but simply put, I think you're a badass. I'm so happy that I was lucky enough to cross paths with you. Thank you for everything you've done over the years. 💪
