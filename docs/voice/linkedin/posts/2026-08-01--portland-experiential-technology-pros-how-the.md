Portland experiential technology pros, how the heck did none of you tell me about CETI?!? 🤬

If you don't know CETI, they are a non-profit organization that focuses on emerging technologies, creative workshops, and interactive public art exhibits.

I got to take a tour of their new space, and MY GOODNESS Y'ALL!!! It's basically an experience agency that you can use for free.

Let me say that again: this is all of the hardware of an experience agency that, somehow, you can use...

...for free.

Want VR helmets? They've got that.
3D printers? Covered.
Soldering tools? Of course.
NUCs? Yup.
Projectors? Yuuuuuup.
Tablets? Duh.
LED Walls? More like Y-E-S walls.

Think about how lucky we are to have these folks in our city. I mean, I'm looking around the room and I see recreational creative techs, pros, networkers, connecters - folks of all types, connected by a neon strobing chain:

The passion we all have for creative technology. 🔥

So I'm hooked. I can't wait to see what I can do to help this community out. The opportunities CETI gives folks can only be replicated by college or work experience, typically, and I'll say this louder for the folks in the back:

IT'S FREE!!!

There will be another meeting in August to talk about the art show they'll be having later on in the year. I think there are real opportunities for local pros and their companies to get involved and make a huge impact on the local community. I believe this could have a ripple effect that would help PDX get its experiential tech swagger back.
