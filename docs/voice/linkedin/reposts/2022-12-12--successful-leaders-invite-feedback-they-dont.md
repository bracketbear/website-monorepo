Successful leaders invite feedback.

They don't run away from it.

They run towards it.

Successful leaders are healthy debate makers.

They don't fight people. They fight ideas even when it's their own.

Here are three questions to invite feedback from your team and supercharge your ideas.

1. "What's the downside to my approach?"

2. "What's a different perspective than mine?"

3. "Can someone please poke holes in my argument?"

If you want to create a high-performing team, reward the best ideas, not the loudest people.
