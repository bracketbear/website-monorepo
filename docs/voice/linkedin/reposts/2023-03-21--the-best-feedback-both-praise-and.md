The best feedback (both praise and criticism) I’ve gotten in my life generally happened in super-quick conversations between meetings or standing waiting for a light to change.

Getting and giving impromptu feedback is more like brushing and flossing than getting a root canal. Don’t schedule it.

Just ask for it and offer it consistently and immediately when it’s needed, and maybe you won’t ever have to get a root canal. Learn more: https://bit.ly/3yqClQC
